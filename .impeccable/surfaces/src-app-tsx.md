Scope: the running instrument — camera stage, HUD, icon rail, settings panel, walkthrough. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience: the player mid-performance, hands in the air, watching a mirrored feed of themselves. Task: hold chords, sweep the filter, switch sections, and configure 480 chords, an envelope, a filter and a six-effect rack. Constraints: audio outranks the interface and nothing may gate it; the panel overlays rather than squeezing; every changing number is tabular in a reserved width; 24px target floor.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The interface is outboard gear around a live monitor. It refuses glassmorphic cards floating over the video, and it refuses expression that obscures state.

OWN-WORLD: The same panel as the start screen — warm-grey painted metal, recessed wells, brushed trim, silkscreened small-caps legends in one grotesk. Colour is strictly a function code: the two hand inks, and the rack's own coded ranks. Grey at rest, colour when live, with line weight carrying state wherever colour cannot.

STORY: The player reads their own hands' state off the meter bridge without looking away from the feed, and finds any control by its silkscreened legend.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The camera feed is the monitor inset in the console, held in a bezel rather than bleeding to the edges. The HUD is the meter bridge along its lower rail: five channel strips with scribble-strip chord names, a ladder meter, and the filter as a panel dial with its legend. The rail is a column of rack buttons; the settings panel slides in as the outboard rack beside the monitor, groups stacked as rack units.

FORM: 1980s studio console and synth front panel. User-pinned, beats the roll. Inherits the start screen's world; same translation from assigned direction 7 (concert programme / event score) — channel strip for movement list, panel legend for event score, rack rail for the ruled spine. Seed key 1f370d22.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
